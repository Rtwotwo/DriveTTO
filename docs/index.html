01 / OVERVIEW

A better plan.
The same planner.

A frozen driving planner still leaves room to improve its trajectory at inference time.

DriveTTO searches around the planner’s output, proposing complete trajectories through endpoint search and temporal fusion. It scores the candidates alongside a preserved reference and returns the selected trajectory, without updating the planner’s parameters.

We examine this approach in NAVSIM evaluation and HUGSIM closed-loop driving. The results distinguish the full strategy, search variants, and benchmark-specific configurations, together with their runtime and transfer behavior.

Controlled comparison · three seeds, fixed model resources +

12,146 observed NAVTEST scenes, 44 parent logs, three search seeds. Full reaches 95.5116 PDMS, improving over CEM8 by 0.4075 (95% CI [0.1149, 0.7274]; Holm-adjusted p = .036).

Isolated p50 latency is 1.3093 s for Full and 1.1998 s for CEM8 (+9.1%). All timings include the common dual-model encoding; the Native row is not a standalone single-model deployment latency.

After averaging seeds within scenes, drops exceeding 20 points from Native occur in 118/12,146 Full scenes and 11/12,146 CEM8 scenes. Minimum, mean and DrivoR terminal ranking show no resolved mean-score difference; this comparison does not isolate temporal fusion.
